t
We are formulating a Token-Level Modality Discrepancy Index (TMDI) leveraging cross-attention maps and Gaussian region uncertainty. By
t

t

The TMDI metric feeds directly into a noise-gated router that dynamically directs clean tokens to specialized text experts while routing corrupted
t
