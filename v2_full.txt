2 Problem Statement
Parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods, particularly Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) [5], have become the
standard approach for adapting large pre-trained models to downstream tasks with minimal memory and
compute overhead. However, real-world document understanding frequently encounters structured input
corruptionΓÇöOCRerrors,missingwords,andlayoutdistortionsΓÇöthatcandegradePEFTperformance.
Standard LoRA applies the same low-rank adaptation uniformly across all layers and samples. While
thisworkswellforcleaninputs,itcanoverfittonoisepatternswhentheinputitselfiscorrupted,leadingto
poorgeneralizationoncleantestdata.
ResearchQuestion. CanwemakeLoRArobusttoinputcorruptionwithoutsacrificingparameter
efficiencyorclean-dataperformance?

Key distinction. Existing PEFT literature has largely focused on robustness to label noise (e.g., De-
LoRA [1], CleaR [2], LoPE [3]), domain shift, or federated learning settings [4]. Comparatively fewer
studies investigate parameter-efficient adaptation under structured OCR input corruption in document un-
derstanding. Input corruption is spatially localized ΓÇö only a subset of tokens is affected ΓÇö and requires
different mitigation strategies than label-noise-robust methods. SALLoRA addresses this gap by operating
at the token level, scaling adaptation per layer, and applying soft sensitivity masking to preserve model
capacitywhiledampeningnoise.
