10 Conclusion
WehaveintroducedSALLoRA,aPEFTframeworkthatcombineslinearprobingwarm-up,layer-wiselearn-
ablescaling,token-levelqualityweighting,andsoftsensitivitymaskingtoachieverobustdocumentunder-
standing under OCR corruption. Under the experimental configuration described in Section 5, SALLoRA
consistently outperformed the Standard LoRA baseline across all evaluated OCR corruption levels, with
the gap widening as the noise level rises. Our ablation study confirms that each component contributes
positively,withsoftsensitivitymaskingandtoken-levelqualityweightingprovidingthelargestgains.
While existing PEFT literature has largely focused on label noise or domain shift, SALLoRA investi-
gatesrobustnesstostructuredinputcorruptionΓÇöadirectionthathasreceivedcomparativelylimitedatten-
tion. Ourresultssuggestthattoken-levelqualityweightingandsoftgradientmaskingareeffectivestrategies
formitigatingspatially-localizedinputcorruptionwithoutsacrificingparameterefficiency.
10.1 FutureWork
The following directions are motivated by our current findings but have not yet been experimentally vali-
dated:
ΓÇó Learnedqualityestimator. Thetoken-levelmaskcurrentlyreliesonground-truthknowledgefromour
synthetic pipeline. Developing a lightweight estimator that predicts token quality from hidden states
wouldenabledeploymentonrealOCRoutputswithoutground-truthlabels.
ΓÇó Adaptive routing. Extending SALLoRA to dynamically route tokens or samples to different adapter
experts(inspiredbyCleaR-stylerouting[2])couldfurtherimproverobustnessunderheterogeneouscor-
ruption.
ΓÇó Multi-expert PEFT (UniMoE-PEFT). Combining SALLoRA with mixture-of-experts architectures
could allow the model to specialize different adapters for different corruption types (substitution, dele-
tion,insertion).
ΓÇó Cross-datasetandcross-architecturevalidation. Evaluatingonadditionalbenchmarks(CORD,SROIE,
DocVQA)andbackbones(Donut,DocFormer)willbeessentialtoestablishgeneralizability.
ΓÇó Real-world OCR evaluation. Testing on outputs from real OCR engines (Tesseract, Google Vision)
willvalidateperformanceunderpracticaldeploymentconditions.
Thesedirectionsrepresentmotivatedextensionsofthecurrentwork,notyetvalidatedcontributions. We
